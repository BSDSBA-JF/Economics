# Study Roadmap

This repository contains a collection of loosely related projects across Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, and Real Analysis. These are grouped together for convenience, though they serve different purposes.

## Overview

The projects fall into two categories:
- Resources intended for future students
- Personal study materials

## Microeconomics

Goal: Build a question database for future students.

- Compile a comprehensive collection of questions across topics
- Include clear and complete solutions
- Serve as a practice and review resource for others

## Macroeconomics

Goal: Provide structured notes for future students.

- Write clear, organized notes for each chapter
- Emphasize key models, assumptions, and intuitions
- Highlight relationships between variables and policy tools
- Present material in a way that is accessible for review and learning

## Real Analysis

Goal: Support my own study and understanding.

- Write full solutions to exercises (primarily from Tao)
- Focus on first-principles proofs
- Revise solutions as my understanding improves
- Track progress by chapter and section

## Notes

- These projects are independent and may vary in style and completeness
- Materials intended for others aim for clarity and usability
- Real Analysis content is primarily for personal use and may be less polished
- The structure may evolve over time
